Blue Whales

Scientific Name: Balaenoptera Musculus

Size: 82-105(ft) | 25-32(m)

Weight: 190-200 metric tons

Life Span: 80-90 years

About Blue Whales

Blue whales are marine mammals and in fact are known as the largest animal to have ever existed in the world due to the proportion of their sheer size and weight. Their diet consists of majority krill and other small aquatic animals that they catch using their baleen plates which replaces teeth. Baleen allows whale to catch krill more efficiently. The Whales gulp a massive amount of water containing lots of krill and then push the water out with it's tongue and trapping the krill inside.

Blue Whales live in all major ocean regions except for the arctic which we will get into in a bit. They typically are found in the continental shelves and tend to migrate frequently. They live in cold oceans where krill is more populated and migrate to waters with warmer temperatures to mate or taking care of their calves. When blue whales aren't mating, their only job is to eat and eat in order to prepare for mating season. The reason why Blue Whals aren't found in the Arctic ocean is because krill populations are too low for their needs.
