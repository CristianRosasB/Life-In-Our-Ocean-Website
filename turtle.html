Green Sea Turtles

Scientific Name: Chelonia mydas

Size: 3-5(ft) | 0.90-1.5(m)

Weight: 300-700(lbs) | 136-318(kg)

Life Span: 50+ years

About Green Sea Turtles

Green Sea Turtles are marine reptiles and are on the bigger side of the sea turtle species. Their paddle-like limbs give them a huge disadvantage when walking on land, but they make up for it as they are known to be great swimmers. They are herbivores and mostly eat seagrass and algae but during the early stages of their life, they can eat crabs, and jellyfish.

Green Sea Turtles are found in tropical oceans typically in shallow waters of the Pacific, Atlantic, and Indian oceans. Coral reefs, and seagrass beds are some examples. Females lay their eggs in areas called nesting beaches which they come back to every 2-5 years to lay eggs. Green sea turtles lay about 110 eggs in total during mating season and they do this every 2 weeks in the span of a couple months. After 2 months the hatchlings come out of their eggs, making their way to the waters. In the mean time they will live in open ocean and go back to waters near shore when they get older.
